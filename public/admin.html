2. Bang tra cuu Cong thuc Toan hoc KaTeX

Viet cong thuc trong cap dau $...$ cho dang inline hoac $$...$$ cho dang khoi o giua dong.

Y nghia Cach viet trong JSON Ket qua hien thi
Phan so $\\frac{a}{b}$
Hon so $2\\frac{1}{3}$
Can bac hai $\\sqrt{x}$
Can bac ba $\\sqrt[3]{x}$
Luy thua (mu) $x^{2}$
Chi so duoi $a_{n}$
Nhan va Chia $a \\times b \\div c$
So sanh $x \\le 5, y \\ge 3, a \\neq b$
So Pi va Do $\\pi, 90^\\circ$
Luu y ve dinh dang:
  • Dau gach cheo nguoc trong JSON: Hay viet hai dau gach cheo \\frac thay vi mot dau. He thong cung co bo loc tu dong sua neu ban quen.
  • Cau tu luan so (number): Thi sinh duoc phep go cac dang tuong duong nhu so nguyen, thap phan (dau . hoac ,), phan so a/b hoac hon so 2 1/3. He thong se tu dong quy doi va tinh dung.
  • Ky tu $ thong thuong: Neu muon ghi ky tu do la that khong phai toan hoc, hay viet \$.

Nhap cau hoi tu JSON

Dan noi dung JSON vao o ben duoi. He thong se tu dong dien tieu de va thoi gian lam bai neu co trong JSON. (Xem mau JSON)
