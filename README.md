# Safe Exam System (safe-seb)

He thong kiem tra bai thi an toan voi co che khoa man hinh sau khi nop bai va dong bo ket qua ve Supabase.

## Muc tieu
- Ngan chan thoat ung dung hoac chuyen man hinh trong va sau khi lam bai.
- Dam bao toan ven du lieu bai thi truoc khi khoa man hinh.
- Dong bo diem so, thoi gian nop bai len he thong co so du lieu tu xa.
- Chi cho phep thoat man hinh khi co xac nhan hoac mat khau tu giam thi.

## Luong hoat dong
1. Thi sinh truy cap vao he thong bai thi.
2. Thi sinh hoan thanh cac cau hoi va bam nop bai.
3. He thong gui du lieu len Supabase va doi xac nhan thanh cong.
4. Man hinh chuyen sang trang thai khoa cung, hien thi thong bao da nop bai.
5. Giam thi kiem tra diem so tu xa va nhap mat khau de giai phong thiet bi.

## Cau hinh moi truong (.env)
- SUPABASE_URL: Dia chi ket noi du an Supabase.
- SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY: Khoa truy cap API cong khai.
- SUPABASE_DB_PASSWORD: Mat khau co so du lieu PostgreSQL.
- PROCTOR_UNLOCK_PASSWORD: Mat khau giam thi dung de mo khoa man hinh.
- PORT: Cong chay may chu noi bo.
